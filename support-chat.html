How do I connect my social media accounts?
Go to Settings > Connected Accounts and click on the platform you want to connect. Follow the authorization process.
Can I schedule posts for multiple platforms at once?
Yes! When creating a post, you can select multiple platforms and the content will be optimized for each one.
How does the AI content generation work?
Our AI uses advanced language models to generate engaging content based on your industry, audience, and preferences.
What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes 30 scheduled posts per month, basic analytics, and AI content generation for up to 10 posts.
