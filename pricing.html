Why Choose Scheduler?

Scheduler is built specifically for Indian businesses, understanding your unique needs and challenges. We offer affordable, transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?
No! Start your 14-day free trial without providing any payment information. You'll only be charged when you decide to continue after the trial.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will be reflected in your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and popular Indian wallets. Payment integration coming soon!
Is my data secure?
Absolutely! We use Firebase for secure data storage with enterprise-grade encryption. Your data is protected and backed up regularly.
